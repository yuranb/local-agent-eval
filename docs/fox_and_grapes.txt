The Fox and the Grapes
From "The Aesop for Children" (1919), public domain

A Fox one day spied a beautiful bunch of ripe grapes hanging from a vine
trained along the branches of a tree. The grapes seemed ready to burst with
juice, and the Fox's mouth watered as he gazed longingly at them.

The bunch hung from a high branch, and the Fox had to jump for it. The first
time he jumped he missed it by a long way. He walked back a few paces, took a
run and jumped again, but still he missed.

He turned and turned, jumping until he was tired, but the grapes were always
just out of reach. Giving up at last, he walked away with his nose in the air,
saying: "I am sure they are sour."

It is easy to despise what you cannot get.
